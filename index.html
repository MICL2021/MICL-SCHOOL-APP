🏫 About Our School

Modern Institute of Creative Learning (MICL)

Modern Institute of Creative Learning (MICL) is committed to providing quality education in a safe, caring and inspiring environment. Our aim is to help every student develop academically, socially, creatively and morally.

🎯 Our Vision

To create confident, responsible and successful students who are prepared to meet the challenges of the future.

📚 Our Mission

To provide quality education through dedicated teachers, modern learning methods and a supportive school environment.

👨‍🏫 Our Teachers

Our dedicated teaching staff works closely with students to support their learning, discipline, confidence and overall development.

🌟 Student Development

We encourage academic excellence along with creativity, discipline, communication skills, sports and other co-curricular activities.

🏛️ School Recognition

Recognised by the Government of Jammu & Kashmir and affiliated with the J&K Board of School Education (JKBOSE).

🤝 Our Commitment

We believe that every child has unique potential. MICL is committed to providing students with the guidance, opportunities and environment they need to grow and succeed.
